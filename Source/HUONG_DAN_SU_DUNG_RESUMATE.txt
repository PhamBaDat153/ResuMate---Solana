HUONG DAN SU DUNG RESUMATE
==========================

Phien ban tai lieu: 1.0
Ung dung: ResuMate - Resume and Credential Platform
Mang blockchain: Solana (network phu thuoc vao cau hinh trien khai cua ung dung)

1. GIOI THIEU
-------------

ResuMate la nen tang quan ly CV va chung nhan nghe nghiep. Ung dung ket hop:

- Ho so va cac phien ban CV.
- Chung nhan (credential) do don vi uy tin cap.
- Xac minh tinh toan ven cua CV/chung nhan bang hash tren Solana.
- Credential cong khai co the xac minh bang hash tren Solana.
- AI danh gia CV theo mo ta cong viec.
- AI tim kiem cong viec phu hop voi CV.

Blockchain luu cac thong tin xac minh, trang thai, hash, URI va quan he quyen han.
Noi dung file CV, claims chi tiet va goi tai lieu ma hoa duoc luu off-chain.

Luu y quan trong:
- Du lieu da ghi tren Solana co the duoc doc qua RPC.
- Truong "Rieng tu" cua resume chi la metadata cho giao dien, khong phai co che
  ma hoa tuyet doi.
- CV duoc upload phien ban hien tai co the nam tren public URL. Bat ky ai co URL
  deu co the tai file.
- Khong chia se seed phrase, private key, passphrase ma hoa hoac link chia se cho
  nguoi khac.

2. CHUAN BI TRUOC KHI SU DUNG
-----------------------------

2.1. Cai dat vi Solana

Can co mot vi Solana ho tro Wallet Standard, vi du mot vi trinh duyet tuong thich
voi Solana. Vi duoc dung de:

- Ket noi tai khoan vao ResuMate.
- Ky giao dich blockchain.
- Thanh toan phi giao dich va rent khi tao account on-chain.
- Thanh toan cac dich vu AI neu ung dung yeu cau x402.

Khong nhap seed phrase vao ResuMate. Ung dung chi yeu cau vi ky giao dich thong
qua cua so xac nhan cua vi.

2.2. Nap SOL

Vi can co du SOL tren dung network de tra:

- Phi giao dich Solana.
- Rent khi tao profile, resume, version, credential, grant hoac account lien quan.
- Phi dich vu AI neu co hien thi yeu cau thanh toan.

Neu dang dung devnet/testnet, hay dung SOL test tu faucet phu hop. Khong gui SOL
mainnet vao dia chi cua mang test.

2.3. Chon dung network

Kiem tra network cua vi trung voi network ma ung dung dang cau hinh. Loi RPC,
blockhash, khong tim thay program hoac khong tim thay account thuong do chon sai
network hoac RPC.

3. BAT DAU VA DIEU HUONG
------------------------

3.1. Ket noi vi

1. Mo website ResuMate.
2. Tai man hinh "Ket noi de vao khong gian cua ban", chon vi Solana.
3. Chap thuan yeu cau ket noi trong cua so vi.
4. Cho ung dung kiem tra tai khoan on-chain.
5. Sau khi ket noi thanh cong, dia chi vi rut gon se hien o thanh tren.

Neu khong thay vi:
- Kiem tra extension vi da cai dat va dang mo khoa.
- Tai lai trang.
- Kiem tra vi co ho tro Wallet Standard hay khong.
- Kiem tra dung mang Solana.

3.2. Ngat ket noi

Nhan "Ngat ket noi" o thanh tren hoac trong tung trang. Viec nay chi ngat ket
noi giao dien, khong xoa du lieu da ghi tren blockchain va khong huy credential.

3.3. Cac khu vuc chinh

- Tong quan: Trang tong hop va dieu huong.
- Ho so cua toi: Tao profile, tao resume, upload va cong bo version CV, xem
  credential.
- Quyen truy cap tai lieu: Xem grant va tao/thu hoi link chia se.
- Bao mat tai lieu: Tao encryption identity, dang ky public key, backup va xoay khoa.
- Tim viec bang AI: Tim cong viec phu hop voi CV.
- Danh gia CV: So sanh CV voi mo ta cong viec.
- Xac minh chung nhan: Kiem tra credential va giai ma tai lieu duoc cap quyen.
- Cong cap chung nhan: Chi danh cho vi issuer dang ky va dang active.
- Quan tri don vi cap: Chi danh cho Registry Authority.

Mot so menu chi hien khi vi da co profile, credential, quyen issuer hoac quyen
Registry Authority tuong ung.

4. TAO HO SO CA NHAN
--------------------

4.1. Tao profile

1. Ket noi vi.
2. Mo "Ho so cua toi".
3. Neu hien thong bao "Vi nay chua co ho so", nhan "Tao ho so".
4. Xem va xac nhan giao dich trong vi.
5. Cho giao dich duoc xac nhan.

Profile luu dia chi owner va cac bo dem resume/credential. Profile khong luu
thong tin ca nhan chi tiet.

Neu tao that bai:
- Tu choi ky: mo lai thao tac va chap thuan ky.
- Thieu SOL: nap them SOL cho phi va rent.
- Profile da ton tai: tai lai trang.
- Loi RPC/network: chon dung network va thu lai.

4.2. Tao resume container

1. Trong trang "Ho so cua toi", den muc "Tao CV".
2. Nhan "Tao resume".
3. Ky giao dich.
4. Cho ung dung xac minh Resume PDA.

Buoc nay chi tao container resume on-chain. Chua co file CV, hash hoac URI.
Noi dung CV duoc them o buoc cong bo phien ban.

4.3. Cong bo phien ban CV

1. Chon resume muon cap nhat.
2. Tai file CV dinh dang PDF hoac DOCX, toi da 10 MB.
3. Tich vao o xac nhan rang file va URI co the cong khai.
4. Nhan "Chuan bi phien ban".
5. Cho ung dung tinh content hash, metadata hash va upload file.
6. Kiem tra thong tin version, URI va hash duoc hien thi.
7. Nhan "Ky va cong bo".
8. Xac nhan giao dich trong vi.
9. Cho giao dich duoc xac minh on-chain.

Moi lan cong bo tao mot version moi, khong ghi de version cu. Hash dung de kiem
tra file tai ve co dung voi file da cong bo hay khong.

Neu version bi stale hoac xung dot, tai lai trang thai resume, chuan bi lai file
va ky lai. Khong tu dong gui lai mot version da thay doi.

4.4. Quan ly CV va credential

Trong trang profile, nguoi dung co the:
- Chon resume khac.
- Xem version tiep theo.
- Xem version da cong bo va hash.
- Xem credential da nhan.
- Nhan "Xac nhan" voi credential dang Active nhung chua duoc subject chap nhan.

5. BAO MAT TAI LIEU VA ENCRYPTION IDENTITY
-------------------------------------------

Encryption identity la cap khoa ma hoa luu tren trinh duyet, tach biet voi khoa
ky giao dich cua vi Solana.

5.1. Tao encryption identity

1. Mo "Bao mat tai lieu".
2. Nhap passphrase toi thieu 8 ky tu.
3. Nhap lai passphrase.
4. Nhan nut tao thiet lap bao mat.
5. Luu passphrase o noi an toan.

Neu mat passphrase hoac xoa du lieu trinh duyet, ban co the khong giai ma duoc
tai lieu da nhan.

5.2. Dang ky public key

1. Ket noi dung vi se dung de nhan/xac minh credential.
2. Dam bao encryption identity da duoc tao.
3. Nhap passphrase.
4. Nhan "Dang ky public key".
5. Neu can, xac nhan thao tac trong vi.
6. Kiem tra trang thai Backend va On-chain.

Public key co the chia se cho issuer. Khong chia se private key hoac passphrase.
Issuer dung public key de ma hoa tai lieu cho nguoi nhan.

5.3. Backup va khoi phuc

- Nhan "Export backup" de tai file backup JSON.
- Cat giu file backup o noi rieng tu, an toan.
- Dung "Import backup" de khoi phuc tren trinh duyet moi.
- Khong gui file backup cho nguoi khong duoc uy quyen.

5.4. Xoay khoa

1. Nhap passphrase hien tai.
2. Nhan "Xoay khoa".
3. Cho key version moi duoc tao.
4. Kiem tra lai trang thai dang ky va cac grant dang hoat dong.

Xoay khoa co the can cap nhat grant. Khong xoa identity cu neu van can giai ma
tai lieu dang ton tai.

6. NHAN VA QUAN LY CHUNG NHAN
-----------------------------

Credential duoc issuer cap cho subject wallet. Credential co the gom loai,
claims, URI tai lieu, hash, thoi han va status.

6.1. Xem credential

1. Mo "Ho so cua toi".
2. Cuon den muc "Chung nhan cua toi".
3. Kiem tra:
   - Ma credential.
   - Don vi cap.
   - Status: Dang hoat dong hoac Da thu hoi.
   - Da duoc subject xac nhan hay chua.
   - Thoi diem het han neu co.
   - URI va cac hash.

6.2. Chap nhan credential

1. Dam bao credential dang Active.
2. Nhan "Xac nhan".
3. Ky giao dich bang subject wallet.
4. Tai lai profile de kiem tra trang thai.

Credential da bi revoke khong the chap nhan lai. Revoke la chuyen trang thai mot
chieu tu Active sang Revoked.

7. QUYEN TRUY CAP TAI LIEU
--------------------------

Mo trang "Quyen truy cap tai lieu" sau khi da ket noi vi va co credential.
Chon credential tu danh sach de xem:

- AccessGrant cho wallet verifier.
- LinkGrant chia se theo link.
- Trang thai, thoi han va so lan su dung.

7.1. Wallet verifier access

AccessGrant cho wallet verifier thuong duoc issuer tao tu dong khi cap
credential. Subject co the xem trang thai grant. Quyen revoke phu thuoc vao
grantor theo chinh sach on-chain; subject khong mac dinh co quyen thu hoi grant
do issuer tao.

7.2. Tao link chia se

Chi dung khi can chia se co thoi han. Link co secret trong phan fragment cua URL.

1. Chon credential.
2. Nhap so ngay het han.
3. Nhap so lan su dung toi da.
4. Nhap Link Grant ID chua dung.
5. Nhap wrapped document key dung dinh dang hex.
6. Nhan "Create Link" va ky giao dich.
7. Sao chep URL duoc tao va gui qua kenh an toan.

Ai co URL va secret co the thu truy cap tai lieu trong pham vi grant. Khong dua
link len noi cong khai.

7.3. Thu hoi link

1. Chon credential va nhap dung Link Grant ID.
2. Nhan "Revoke Link".
3. Xac nhan hop thoai.
4. Ky giao dich.

Ban sao link cu se ngung hoat dong sau khi giao dich thu hoi duoc xac nhan.

8. XAC MINH CHUNG NHAN
----------------------

Credential public khong can AccessGrant, Share Link, vi verifier hoac passphrase.
Verifier chi can Credential address. He thong tai manifest va file cong khai,
tinh lai document hash/claims hash, sau do so sanh voi du lieu tren Solana.

8.1. Xac minh bang wallet

1. Ket noi wallet verifier.
2. Mo "Xac minh chung nhan".
3. Nhap dia chi credential.
4. Nhap mat khau bao mat tai lieu/passphrase theo yeu cau hien thi.
5. Chon hoac bo chon tuy chon "Chi chap nhan chung nhan da duoc nguoi nhan
   xac nhan".
6. Nhan "Xac minh chung nhan".
7. Doc ket qua Verified/Failed va ly do neu that bai.
8. Neu hop le, nhan "Tai tai lieu da xac minh".

Qua trinh xac minh kiem tra quyen truy cap, credential status, issuer, thoi han,
hash tai lieu va claims hash. File tai ve la ban sao tren thiet bi va khong the
bi thu hoi tu xa.

8.2. Xac minh bang link chia se

1. Mo URL chia se day du, bao gom phan sau dau #.
2. Kiem tra link con Active, chua het han va chua vuot max uses.
3. Ket noi wallet khi san sang consume link. Wallet chu yeu tra phi giao dich.
4. Nhan "Consume and verify".
5. Cho giao dich va cac buoc kiem tra hash hoan tat.
6. Tai file neu hien thi ket qua Credential verified.

Neu thieu phan secret trong URL, link da revoke, het han hoac het luot dung,
khong the xac minh.

9. DICH VU AI
-------------

9.1. Danh gia CV

1. Mo "Danh gia CV".
2. Ket noi vi Solana co kha nang ky giao dich.
3. Tai CV PDF hoac DOCX.
4. Chon mot trong hai cach cung cap JD:
   - Dan van ban mo ta cong viec.
   - Tai file JD PDF hoac DOCX.
5. Nhan "Danh gia".
6. Neu duoc yeu cau, xac nhan thanh toan x402 trong vi.
7. Cho AI phan tich va xem ket qua tai trang ket qua.

Khong the gui neu thieu CV, thieu JD hoac wallet chua ket noi. Hay kiem tra ky
thong tin truoc khi ky thanh toan.

9.2. Tim viec bang AI

1. Mo "Tim viec bang AI".
2. Tai CV PDF hoac DOCX, toi da 10 MB.
3. Nhap vi tri mong muon neu co.
4. Nhap dia diem mong muon neu co.
5. Chon hinh thuc Remote, Hybrid, On-site hoac tat ca.
6. Dieu chinh diem phu hop toi thieu.
7. Nhan "Tim viec phu hop".
8. Xac nhan thanh toan neu he thong yeu cau.
9. Cho qua trinh AI hoan tat va xem danh sach cong viec.
10. Nhan "Xem cong viec" de mo tin tuyen dung.

Ket qua gom diem phu hop, cong ty, dia diem, muc luong, ly do phu hop, ky nang
da khop va ky nang con thieu.

10. HUONG DAN CHO ISSUER
------------------------

Issuer la vi duoc Registry Authority phe duyet va dang active.

10.1. Dieu kien

- Vi phai duoc dang ky trong issuer registry.
- Trang thai issuer phai la ACTIVE.
- Subject phai co profile.
- Issuer phai co encryption identity/public key can thiet de ma hoa tai lieu.

10.2. Cap credential

1. Mo "Cong cap chung nhan".
2. Ket noi dung issuer wallet.
3. Nhap public key subject.
4. Nhan thao tac tra cuu subject va dam bao subject co profile.
5. Nhap cac claims theo cap key/value.
6. Nhap credential type.
7. Tai tai lieu credential dang PDF.
8. Neu can, bat expiry va chon ngay het han trong tuong lai.
9. Kiem tra preview hash.
10. Nhan thao tac cap credential.
11. Ma hoa, upload goi credential va ky giao dich.
12. He thong tao grant cho subject; tuy chon tao them link chia se.
13. Kiem tra credential moi trong danh sach issuer.

Credential document phai la PDF. Khong dung URI qua dai gioi han on-chain.

10.3. Thu hoi credential

1. Trong danh sach credential da cap, chon credential can thu hoi.
2. Nhan "Revoke".
3. Ky giao dich.
4. Xac nhan status chuyen sang Revoked.

Chi issuer goc da cap credential moi co quyen thu hoi credential do.

11. HUONG DAN CHO REGISTRY AUTHORITY
------------------------------------

Registry Authority la vi quan tri danh sach issuer.

11.1. Khoi tao registry

1. Mo "Quan tri don vi cap".
2. Ket noi vi muon lam authority.
3. Neu registry chua ton tai, nhan "Khoi tao registry".
4. Ky giao dich.

Wallet dau tien khoi tao se tro thanh authority. Hay thuc hien can than vi quyen
nay la goc tin cay cua danh sach issuer.

11.2. Dang ky issuer

1. Nhap public key cua issuer.
2. Chon loai issuer:
   - 1: Truong dai hoc.
   - 2: Doanh nghiep.
   - 3: To chuc chung nhan.
   - 4: Co quan nha nuoc.
   - 5: Hiep hoi nghe nghiep.
3. Nhan tra cuu de kiem tra issuer.
4. Nhan dang ky issuer.
5. Ky giao dich.

Issuer khong can tu ky giao dich dang ky; authority la nguoi phe duyet.

11.3. Bat/tat issuer

1. Tra cuu issuer.
2. Kiem tra status hien tai.
3. Nhan nut bat hoac tam dung.
4. Ky giao dich.

Issuer INACTIVE khong duoc cap credential moi. Theo chinh sach hien tai, issuer
van co the thu hoi credential do minh da cap.

12. XU LY LOI THUONG GAP
------------------------

12.1. Khong ket noi duoc vi

- Mo khoa extension vi.
- Kiem tra vi co ho tro Wallet Standard.
- Tai lai trang va ket noi lai.
- Kiem tra dung network.

12.2. Tu choi ky giao dich

Day khong nhat thiet la loi ung dung. Mo lai thao tac, doc ky noi dung giao dich
va chap thuan trong vi neu ban chac chan.

12.3. Khong du SOL

Nap SOL dung network. Can du ca phi giao dich va rent tao account. Khong thu lai
nhieu lan lien tuc khi chua bo sung so du.

12.4. Loi RPC, blockhash, khong tim thay program

- Kiem tra network cua vi va ung dung.
- Tai lai trang de lay blockhash moi.
- Kiem tra RPC dang hoat dong.
- Thu lai sau mot khoang thoi gian ngan.

12.5. Profile/resume bi trung hoac xung dot

Tai lai du lieu on-chain bang nut thu lai/trang tai lai. Khong tu nhap resume ID
khac; ID duoc cap tuan tu theo profile.

12.6. Upload that bai

- Kiem tra dinh dang PDF/DOCX theo tung chuc nang.
- Kiem tra kich thuoc file.
- Kiem tra ket noi mang va storage.
- Chon lai file va thuc hien lai buoc chuan bi.

12.7. Khong giai ma duoc credential

- Kiem tra dang dung dung trinh duyet/identity.
- Nhap dung passphrase.
- Kiem tra public key va key version da dang ky.
- Khong xoa local storage neu van can tai lieu.
- Neu da xoay khoa, kiem tra grant co duoc cap nhat hay chua.

12.8. Link chia se khong hoat dong

Kiem tra:
- URL co con phan #secret hay khong.
- Link co bi revoke khong.
- Link co het han khong.
- So lan su dung co vuot max uses khong.
- Link Grant ID va credential address co dung khong.
- Wallet da ket noi de consume va tra phi chua.

12.9. Xac minh that bai

Ly do co the gom credential bi revoke, issuer khong active, credential het han,
subject chua chap nhan, khong co grant, sai passphrase, sai link, document hash
khong khop hoac claims hash khong khop. Doc message chi tiet tren man hinh va
khong bo qua canh bao integrity.

13. NGUYEN TAC AN TOAN
----------------------

- Chi ky giao dich ban hieu ro va dung vi.
- Khong cung cap seed phrase/private key cho bat ky ai.
- Khong dua passphrase ma hoa vao form khong ro nguon goc.
- Luu backup identity o noi an toan, ma hoa neu co the.
- Khong cong khai share link co secret.
- Khong upload thong tin nhay cam vao CV public.
- Kiem tra issuer, status, expiry va hash truoc khi tin credential.
- Nho rang hash chi chung minh file/claims khong bi thay doi; khong tu dong
  chung minh noi dung la su that.
- File da tai ve may khong the bi thu hoi tu xa.
- Kiem tra lai dia chi vi va network truoc moi thao tac co phi.
